OUR SOLUTION

Scientific Groundwater Recharge

We combine hydrogeology, resistivity surveys, AI-assisted analysis and modular engineering to recharge groundwater scientifically, efficiently and sustainably.

HOW WE SOLVE IT

Science + Technology + Engineering

Ecohydra identifies where groundwater can be recharged effectively and then designs a site-specific modular recharge system.

01

Hydrogeology

We study the geological and hydrogeological conditions of the site to understand how groundwater moves beneath the surface.

02

Resistivity Survey

Geophysical resistivity surveys help identify subsurface formations and potential recharge zones without unnecessary excavation.

03

AI-Assisted Analysis

Site data is analysed using advanced tools to support identification of suitable groundwater recharge locations.

04

Modular Engineering

We design customized modular recharge systems according to the site's geological conditions, water availability and recharge requirements.
