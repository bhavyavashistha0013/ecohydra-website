OUR SERVICES

Complete Groundwater Recharge Solutions

From scientific groundwater investigation to installation, monitoring and maintenance, Ecohydra provides end-to-end recharge solutions.

WHAT WE DO

Services Built Around Your Site

Every project begins with understanding the site and ends with a practical, measurable groundwater recharge solution.

01

Groundwater Investigation

Scientific assessment of geological and hydrogeological conditions to understand groundwater behaviour and recharge potential.

02

Resistivity Surveys

Geophysical surveys to study subsurface formations and identify suitable zones for groundwater recharge.

03

Recharge System Installation

Professional installation of customized modular recharge systems based on the scientific assessment of the site.

04

Groundwater Monitoring

Monitoring and assessment of recharge performance to help understand the impact of implemented solutions.

05

Maintenance & After-Sales

Continued support and maintenance to help keep recharge systems performing effectively over time.

06

Customized Recharge Solutions

Site-specific recharge systems designed according to local water availability, geology and project requirements.
