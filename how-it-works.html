OUR PROCESS

How It Works

From understanding the subsurface to installing a customized recharge system, Ecohydra follows a scientific and structured process.

SIX STEPS

From Investigation to Recharge

Every Ecohydra project is designed around the unique hydrogeological conditions of the site.

01

Investigate

We conduct a detailed site assessment and study the geological and hydrogeological conditions.

02

Analyze

Field observations and geophysical data are analysed to understand subsurface conditions.

03

Identify

Suitable groundwater recharge zones are identified using scientific site-specific analysis.

04

Design

A customized modular recharge system is designed according to the site's specific requirements.

05

Install

The modular recharge system is professionally installed at the selected recharge location.

06

Monitor

Recharge performance can be monitored and supported through maintenance and after-sales services.
